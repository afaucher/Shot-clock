# Reference photos and videos

Collected 19 Nov 2026 to show what shots usually look like. The app's repo is public, so only openly licensed photos are copied in (`img/reference/`, credits in [img/reference/CREDITS.md](../img/reference/CREDITS.md)). They appear in the shot report under "Reference photos", and report notes link to the matching photo.
